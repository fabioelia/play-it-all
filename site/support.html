Support

Find a fix below, or get in touch.

Something isn't skipping

Nothing happens on a streaming site

Open the CleanCut popup and check that a rating other than Uncut is selected and the switch at the top is on. Then check the status line. If it says subtitles not scanned yet, turn on English captions once so the player downloads the subtitle file. Reload the page after installing or updating the extension.

A scene played that shouldn't have

Some scenes have no catalog tag and no revealing subtitle line, so there's nothing to act on. Flag it yourself: move the mouse over the player and click Flag scene where it starts and Done where it ends (or Last 10 s right after it), or use Alt+[ and Alt+]. Then use Share on GitHub in the popup so everyone benefits.

Skips land a bit early or late

Some services' clocks drift from the programme time, especially with ad breaks. Use the Sync nudge in the popup to shift timing for that title. Where a title's tags were timed on a different service, CleanCut says so and tries to align them automatically.

It worked yesterday and doesn't today

Streaming sites change their pages without notice, which can break detection until we update. Please report it with the details below. When a skip can't be performed, CleanCut falls back to muting and hiding the picture for that scene.

There are no community tags for my show

The catalog is new, so many titles aren't covered yet. CleanCut still scans the subtitles, and you can flag scenes yourself and share them. Contributing guide.

It doesn't work on my streaming service at all

Most services are built against their current players but not yet verified with live accounts, so a report from a real viewer is valuable. Please tell us the service and what you see (see below).

Report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/fabioelia/play-it-all/issues. Please include:

  • Your browser and version, and the CleanCut version (shown in chrome://extensions).
  • The service and the title/episode, and what you expected to happen.
  • What the popup showed: community tags, scan results or an error.

It helps to tick Record a debug log in the popup, reproduce the problem, and click Save debug log. The log contains page and network details, so read it before posting. Please don't send subtitle files or account passwords. We never need them.

Delete my data

Uninstalling removes everything on your device. There are no accounts. Tags and skip lists you contributed are public files in the catalog; to have one removed, open an issue with the title and we'll delete it.
