    Questions.

    Why does it say “subtitles not scanned yet”?

    The scan uses the English subtitle file the player downloads. Turn on English captions once and the scan runs. You can hide the captions again afterwards.

    Does it see the video frames?

    No. Streaming video is protected, so extensions can’t read the picture. CleanCut works from the catalog and the subtitles, so it can miss a scene that has neither. That’s why community flags matter, and why machine-detected scenes are treated as suggestions until you trust them.

    Why do skips land late on some services?

    Where ads are stitched into the stream, the player’s clock runs ahead of the episode after each ad break. CleanCut shows only its badge during ads, and the popup has a Sync nudge for timing that’s off.

    Who checks the community tags?

    Every tag and skip list is a file submitted through GitHub. A bot validates it and a person reviews it before it’s published. You can read every file in the public catalog.

    Is it legal?

    In the US, the Family Movie Act of 2005 allows skipping and muting parts of a film for private home viewing, as long as no altered copy is made. CleanCut works that way: it never copies, edits or redistributes a video. Streaming services’ terms and laws in other countries differ. This is not legal advice.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. There are no ads, no tracking and no paid tier.
