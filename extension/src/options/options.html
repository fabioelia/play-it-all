CleanCut

Watch any show at the rating you choose. CleanCut skips, mutes or hides scenes using timestamps from a community catalog on GitHub, plus a scan-ahead analysis of the episode's subtitles.

Troubleshooting

If a site feels slow with CleanCut on, switch these off one at a time, reload the site, and see which one makes the difference.

How to tag and share

While watching, press Alt+[ when a scene starts and Alt+] when it ends, then pick a category and severity. Your tags apply right away, for you.

To share them, open the CleanCut popup and click Share on GitHub. It opens a prefilled issue, and a bot turns it into a pull request for the catalog. How the catalog works.

Scan-ahead works best with English subtitles. On Netflix and Prime, CleanCut reads the track the player already downloads. Turn on English [CC] once if nothing gets scanned. Subtitle timing is also how CleanCut lines up tags made on another service.
