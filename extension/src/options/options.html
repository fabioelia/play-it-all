CleanCut

Watch any show at the rating you choose.

CleanCut skips, mutes or hides scenes using timestamps from a community catalog on GitHub, plus a look ahead at the episode’s subtitles. Pick your rating from the CleanCut button in the toolbar while you watch.

How it works

Flag a scene
While watching, open CleanCut and choose Flag a scene on the player, or press Alt+[ when a scene starts and Alt+] when it ends. Your flag applies right away, just for you.
Share with everyone
Open CleanCut while the title is on screen and choose Share on GitHub. It opens a prefilled issue; after a quick review, a bot adds your tags to the catalog. How the catalog works
Subtitles help
Scanning ahead works best with English subtitles. On Netflix and Prime Video, CleanCut reads the track the player already downloads; if nothing gets scanned, turn on English [CC] once. Subtitle timing is also how CleanCut lines up tags made on another service.
